# Movement & Mobility Reference Guide

## 1. Ankle & Lower Leg Mobility (post-sprain recovery)

- **Knee-to-Wall Dynamic Pulses**
  - Focus: ankle dorsiflexion.
  - Execution: foot 5–8 cm from the wall, drive the knee forward to touch the wall without the heel lifting. 10–12 dynamic reps per side.
- **Ankle CARs (Controlled Articular Rotations)**
  - Focus: re-activating the nervous system after an ankle strain.
  - Execution: slow 360° ankle rotations at full range. 5 reps each direction.

## 2. Hip & Pelvis Mobility (kinetic chain integration)

- **90/90 Hip Switches**
  - Focus: internal/external hip rotation, loosening the hip capsule.
  - Execution: sit with legs at 90° angles, switch knee direction without hands. 8–10 reps per side.
- **Cossack Squats**
  - Focus: lateral hip mobility, adductor flexibility, low centre-of-gravity control.
  - Execution: wide stance, shift deep into one side while the other leg stays straight. 6–8 reps per side.
- **World's Greatest Stretch**
  - Focus: full-body opener (hip flexor, hamstring, thoracic spine).
  - Execution: deep lunge, elbow to ankle, thoracic rotation to the ceiling. 5 reps per side.

## 3. Kinetic Chain & Anti-Rotation

- **Drop Squats**
  - Focus: deceleration, dropping the centre of gravity through the hips rather than the upper back.
  - Execution: drop fast into a low squat and stick the landing. 3 × 8.
- **Single-Leg RDL**
  - Focus: single-leg balance, ankle stability, posterior chain.
  - Execution: hinge at the hip with one leg extended back, hips level. 3 × 8–10.
- **Suitcase Carry**
  - Focus: anti-lateral-flexion core stability linking lower body to torso.
  - Execution: heavy single-arm kettlebell or dumbbell walk, 20–30 m.
